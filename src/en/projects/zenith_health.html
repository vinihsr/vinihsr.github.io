Zenith App is a collaborative project built for the Health Tech sector. Its main goal is to modernize and ease medical diagnosis through the digitization and intelligent management of clinical medical histories (anamnesis).

Unlike conventional applications, the healthcare sector demands highly sensitive data handling. For that reason, this project's architecture was designed with a strong emphasis on security, resilience, and legal compliance.

Security and Compliance (LGPD)

Managing patient data raised architectural challenges that were solved by applying the principles of Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD) directly in the system's logic:

  • Terms and Conditions (T&C): Implementation of explicit-consent flows, ensuring that health data is collected and used only with traceable consent.
  • Right to Erasure (Full Deletion): Development of Hard Delete routines, ensuring that, upon request, the user's data and records are cryptographically erased and permanently removed from the database, leaving no transactional residue.

Resilience and Data Governance

Since medical information is critical and cannot be lost, the application's database was structured with preventive policies:

  • Backup & Restore: Implementation of contingency routines, ensuring periodic snapshots and restore tests. This policy gives the application an acceptable recovery time (RTO/RPO) in failure scenarios, preserving the integrity of the clinical history.

This was a team project, consolidating version control practices and agile methodologies.
