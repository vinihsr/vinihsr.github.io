My hands-on experience in software development and engineering was forged at FATEC through the Integrated Project-Based Learning program (known as API, from the Portuguese Aprendizado por Projetos Integrados). Each semester, we were challenged to solve real problems for major companies in the tech market, taking on development and agile management roles.

Below, I document the technical progression of the Mirage Group team across 6 semesters.

1ST SEMESTER (AUG/2022) // FATEC SJC

Laboratory Maintenance Management

Challenge: Map and simplify the opening of technical tickets to identify and repair faulty computers in the institution's labs.

Solution: An interactive web application with drag and drop to visually rearrange the machines in each room and manage their maintenance status.

  • Stack: Python (Flask), MySQL, HTML5, CSS, and JavaScript.
  • My Role: Building and styling the screens (with a focus on responsiveness), and actively contributing to the documentation that underpinned the drag-and-drop logic.
2ND SEMESTER (FEB/2023) // FATEC SJC

Class Management and School Statistics

Challenge: Develop a desktop system to replace an unstable portal, allowing teachers to manage classes, assignments, and grades, and to track statistical performance efficiently.

  • Stack: Java, Java Swing, and MySQL.
  • My Role: Prototyping and developing the graphical interface with Swing, and helping build the logic behind the statistics base. Took an active leadership role in overcoming the team's build and compilation challenges.
3RD SEMESTER (AUG/2023) // IONIC HEALTH

Tracking of Corporate Regulatory Processes

Challenge: Create a unified web platform to manage, document, and monitor the evidence and stages of bureaucratic regulatory processes at a company focused on clinical engineering.

  • Stack: Node.js, TypeScript, React, and MySQL.
  • My Role: Building the application's critical screens (such as login/home) and structuring continuous feedback to guide the project's architectural decisions.
4TH SEMESTER (FEB/2024) // SIATT

Integrated Scheduling Platform (Hybrid)

Challenge: Address the disorganization of growing teams in the post-pandemic context by coordinating appointments that respect different formats (in-person, online, hybrid) and availabilities.

  • Stack: Node.js, TypeScript, React, and MySQL.
  • My Role: Conceiving the MVP, breaking the work down into tasks, and developing the visual side of the meeting registration system.
5TH SEMESTER (AUG/2024) // KERSYS

Georeferenced Weather Monitoring

Challenge: Develop a mobile solution that lets farmers monitor extreme weather conditions in their specific growing areas, sending impact notifications.

  • Stack: React Native, Node.js, Firebase (NoSQL), and MySQL.
  • My Role: Scrum Master. Ran the agile ceremonies using Jira Software. Prioritized the backlog, kept deadlines on track, and shielded the team from blockers by working directly with the Product Owner.
6TH SEMESTER (FEB/2025) // DOM ROCK

RLHF Platform: LLM Retraining

Challenge: A web application to evaluate Artificial Intelligence responses in real time. The system sends prompts to two LLMs, receives the output via streaming, and collects human feedback (RLHF) to retrain a model specialized in care for Alzheimer's patients.

  • Stack: Vue.js, Spring Boot, FastAPI, LangChain, Groq (Qwen/DeepSeek), MongoDB, and ChromaDB.
  • My Role (Developer):
    • MongoDB architectural design geared toward a smooth RLHF flow.
    • Implementation of Streaming Response to reduce LLM latency.
    • Building integrated routes to feed the performance charts and the analytical comparison views.
